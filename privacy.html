3. Why do we use this information?

We use registration details to administer the DARTIQ VIP waiting list, record eligibility for the VIP Founder offer, provide launch and access information, prevent abuse, and attribute registrations to the appropriate ambassador.

4. Legal basis

We process registration details to respond to your request to join the waiting list and, where applicable, based on your consent. We use necessary security information to protect the registration service under our legitimate interests.

Optional promotional marketing beyond information about your requested registration requires a separate appropriate legal basis and, where required, separate consent.

5. Ambassador referrals

If you register through an ambassador link, we save the referral code and campaign identifier. We use these to attribute the registration and evaluate our ambassador programme.

Ambassadors should not receive your personal contact details merely because they referred you. Aggregated referral statistics may be shared with the relevant ambassador.

6. Service providers

The registration website uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for registration storage and processing, and Cloudflare Turnstile for security verification.

These providers may process technical or personal data according to their service arrangements. International transfers, where applicable, must be covered by appropriate data protection safeguards.

7. How long do we retain information?

Waiting-list records are retained while needed to manage the VIP invitation and registration process. Records no longer required are deleted or anonymised according to DARTIQ's retention schedule, subject to applicable legal obligations.

The specific retention schedule must be finalised before public launch.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. Where consent is relied upon, you may withdraw it at any time.

Contact us using the privacy email above. You may also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: www.dataprotectionauthority.be .

9. Payments and free trials

Joining the VIP waiting list does not create a paid subscription. Your seven-day free trial begins only when you activate it after launch. Payment information will be collected separately if you choose a paid subscription.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the registration service or its data processing changes. The latest version will be available on this page.
